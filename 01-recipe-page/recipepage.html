Creamy Veggie Patsa

A delicious and easy creamy veggie pasta loaded with fresh vegetables and rich white sauce, perfect for a quick lunch or dinner.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 1 cup chopped bell peppers
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese

Instructions:

  1. Saute bell peppers and broccoli in butter for 3–4 minutes.
  2. Add flour, then slowly pour in milk while stirring to make a creamy sauce.
  3. Mix in cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Add boiled pasta to the sauce and mix well.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy.
